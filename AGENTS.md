# AGENTS.md — ChatBeds Marketing Website

## 1. Project Mission

Build the public marketing website for **ChatBeds**, a modern hospitality Property Management System (PMS) with a unique **in-chat operating model**.

ChatBeds is not just a chatbot connected to a PMS. It is a full PMS that includes reservations, rooms, front desk, housekeeping, maintenance, staff, inventory, finance, night audit, revenue management, distribution, guest experience, reports, analytics, and more.

Its key differentiator is that hotel staff can perform and receive operational work through **WhatsApp**.

Example workflow:

1. A guest checks out.
2. ChatBeds updates the room status.
3. The relevant manager and cleaner receive a WhatsApp notification.
4. The cleaner can reply with a command such as `301 clean` or use a voice note.
5. The supervisor is notified for inspection.
6. The room status is updated to Ready inside the PMS.
7. Front desk can immediately see that the room is available.

The website must communicate this clearly.

Core positioning:

> **Your property runs in ChatBeds. Your team runs it from WhatsApp.**

Alternative hero line:

> **The PMS your entire hotel can use — from WhatsApp.**

Do not position ChatBeds as only:
- a WhatsApp bot,
- a guest messaging tool,
- an AI chatbot,
- a booking engine,
- or a lightweight hotel app.

It is a **complete hospitality operating system / PMS with WhatsApp as a frontline operating interface**.

## 6. Product Story To Communicate

The strongest website story is:

Traditional PMS workflow:

**Something happens → employee logs into PMS → finds module → updates status → another employee checks PMS**

ChatBeds workflow:

**Something happens → ChatBeds knows → right person gets a WhatsApp message → staff responds from WhatsApp → PMS updates automatically**

This should be shown visually.

The centerpiece website section should present a clear, static flow similar to:

**Guest checks out**

↓

**Room 301 marked for cleaning**

↓

WhatsApp notification:
> Room 301 is ready for cleaning.

↓

Cleaner:
> 301 clean

↓

Supervisor:
> Room 301 ready for inspection.

↓

**Room 301 → Ready**

↓

**Front Desk sees room availability instantly**

Present the complete workflow with a concise three-stage explanation, a WhatsApp-style conversation and the final PMS room state. Keep every stage and message visible without scroll pinning, step selection or changing messages.

## 8. Visual Direction

The current brand direction is a light, cozy orange, purple and white theme matching the supplied ChatBeds logo. Use this palette for new pages and future edits.

The website should feel:

- Premium
- Modern
- Operational
- Reliable
- Smart
- Hospitality-focused
- Not overly playful
- Not corporate / boring
- Not like a generic AI landing page

Use the visual language of the existing ChatBeds product.

### Brand colors

Base:
- Warm white / off-white backgrounds
- White surfaces with pale lavender and soft peach section accents

Brand:
- Logo: Blue `#1800A2` for “Chat” and the chat bubble; Orange `#D16046` for “Beds” and the bed frame
- Orange for selected interface highlights
- Purple / violet as primary interactive accent

Supporting:
- Dark plum / charcoal text
- Muted plum-gray secondary text
- Green for success states
- Red only for errors / alerts

Do not overuse orange and purple together in every component.

Use one dominant accent per section.

```css
:root {
  --bg: #fffdf9;
  --surface: #ffffff;
  --surface-soft: #f5f1fc;
  --border: #e5deee;

  --text: #2c243a;

  --orange: #ed7a2f;
```

Use dark orange for small orange text and pale mint for success surfaces. Keep text contrast adequate on every light surface. Adjust values to match the current logo and the theme tokens in `app/globals.css`.
